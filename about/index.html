About

Mark, Palm Springs

I'm 58, I live in Palm Springs, and I'm learning watercolor. I paint small desert scenes, mostly 4×6 and 5×7, because that's the size I enjoy.

Most of what I paint is close to home: the San Jacinto Mountains, the skies over Palm Springs, a roadrunner on Sunrise Parkway, the Salton Sea. A few come from imagination.

The originals are available. If one interests you, write to me.

Questions
Are the original paintings for sale?
Yes. Every painting on the site is an original and currently available. Write to mark@desertpaintings.com to ask about one.
How big are the paintings?
They are small: 4 × 6 inches and 5 × 7 inches, painted on watercolor cardstock.
What do you paint?
Mostly the desert around Palm Springs, California: the San Jacinto Mountains, summer and monsoon skies, a roadrunner on Sunrise Parkway, the Salton Sea. A few come from imagination.
Who paints them?
Mark, a 58-year-old in Palm Springs who is learning watercolor and paints small desert scenes.
How are you learning watercolor?
From books and videos: Start to Paint with Watercolours by Arnold Lowrey (Search Press), The Laws Guide to Nature Drawing and Journaling by John Muir Laws (Heyday), Bob Ross, and beginner lessons on YouTube, including videos with Rajiv Surendra and his teacher Marcelo Daldoce.
Where can I watch you paint?
Painting videos are coming to YouTube at @desertpaintings.
